Spine Angles

Quick angle measurements on a photo of a radiograph.

  • Shoot square-on to the screen, or use a screenshot. A tilted photo skews angles.
  • Your photo stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Approximate measurements for convenience, not for diagnostic use.

Automatic landmark guess: getting ready…

Credits

The automatic guess uses a landmark network that was trained on two sets of radiographs:

  • Lateral lumbar spine films of the Burapha Spine Dataset (BUU-LSPINE), Faculty of Informatics, Burapha University, Thailand (services.informatics.buu.ac.th/spine). Klinwichit P. et al., “BUU-LSPINE: A Thai Open Lumbar Spine Dataset for Spondylolisthesis Detection”, Applied Sciences 2023, 13(15), 8646, doi:10.3390/app13158646.
  • Sagittal pelvic radiographs of Chai et al.: the images (figshare, doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.23820879.v1, CC BY 4.0) and their annotations (doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.23831553.v1, CC0), described in Chai Y., Boudali A.M., Maes V., Walter W.L., Scientific Data 2024, doi:10.1038/s41597-024-04003-7.

The network's backbone started from ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-18 weights (torchvision's ResNet-18, as provided by segmentation_models_pytorch). None of these images are part of the app.

Burapha University and the datasets' authors have not reviewed or endorsed this app.
